Frequently asked questions
How much does it cost to charge a Rivian vehicle?

Charging costs vary based on the cost of electricity in your area. In general, when charging at home, you can expect to fully charge a Rivian vehicle for about half the cost of a tank of gas.

You can also program your Rivian Wall Charger to accept charge during off-peak hours when energy rates are most affordable, further lowering your costs.

For public charging, pricing varies as each charging network sets its own price. Pricing often correlates to the charger’s speed and even still, pricing is typically at or below the cost of gasoline. Refer to the charger’s screen for specific pricing details.

Does Rivian offer leasing?

Yes, Rivian offers leasing of select R1T and R1S configurations in select states.

Enter your zip code to view lease-eligible vehicles in your area.

How does delivery work?

Our delivery team is focused on creating a seamless and convenient experience that guides you from configuration to the first time you drive your Rivian — including service center pickups and home deliveries where available.


We'll be in touch to help you prepare for your vehicle, including finalizing your payment method and applying for financing, trading in your current vehicle and completing any remaining documents. When it’s time to make your final payment, you’ll be able to do so from your Rivian account.


On delivery day, you can set up your digital key through the Rivian app to immediately get on the road. To get better acquainted with your vehicle, you can ask a Rivian Specialist for a walkthrough or explore on your own time.

What is the bumper to bumper length?

The bumper-to-bumper length of R1S is 200.8 inches, R1T is 217.1 inches and R2 is 185.9 inches.

Learn more about the specs for R1S, R1T and R2.

What is the vehicle width with the side mirrors folded?

When the side mirrors are folded, the width of R1S and R1T is 81.8 inches and the width of R2 is 78.1 inches.

Learn more about the specs for R1S, R1T and R2.

What is the wheelbase length?

The length of the wheelbase of R1S is 121.1 inches, R1T is 135.8 inches and R2 is 115.6 inches.

Learn more about the specs for R1S, R1T and R2.

What is the water fording height?

Depending on your suspension settings and wheel size, the maximum water fording height of the R1T is 43.1 inches, 43.0 inches for R1S and 19.7 inches for R2 while all the doors and storage spaces are fully closed.

Do not open the vehicle while fording water as this can flood the interior which can damage the vehicle.

What is the maximum towing capacity?

Towing capacity will vary by vehicle and the type of hitch used.

R2

When equipped with the Tow Package, R2 has a towing capacity of 4,400 lbs.

R1

A weight distributing hitch is required to achieve the maximum towing capability of R1S and R1T. Without a weight distributing hitch, the towing capacity is 5,000 lbs.
