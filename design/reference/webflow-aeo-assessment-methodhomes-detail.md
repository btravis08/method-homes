Overall AEO Maturity score: 2 out of 5

Your top 3 priorities

Here's where methodhomes.net can make the fastest gains in AEO visibility, accuracy, and traffic. If you only did three things, do these:

Complete key page SEO metadata: only ~27% of important pages have full title tags, meta descriptions, and h1 headings. Reaching 80%+ coverage on homepage, product, service, about, contact, and FAQ pages is a gate requirement to unlock technical level 2.

Add depth to content pages: many pages average ~125 words, well below the 300-word threshold AI engines need to extract meaningful answers. Expanding key product, service, and FAQ pages is required to move content from level 1 to level 2.

Add author bylines with credential-rich bios and person schema markup to blog posts and articles. This is a gate requirement for authority level 3 and directly strengthens the expertise signals AI platforms use when recommending brands.

AI visibility snapshot
Method Homes is consistently well-represented in consideration-stage queries about best prefab builders in the Pacific Northwest, often appearing as the #1 or #2 recommendation with accurate descriptions of its premium, architect-designed, sustainable positioning. However, the brand is entirely absent from awareness-stage educational content and retention/maintenance guides, resulting in very low overall message pull-through. When mentioned, the brand is portrayed very positively with generally accurate information, but the company's specific differentiators — such as its 400+ completed projects, integrated process with architects and engineers, and founding history — are rarely conveyed.
